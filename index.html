Cyber Escape

Welcome to Cyber Escape, player. Your mission is to collect stolen data from the computer system.

Play Now

How to Play

  1. Use the WASD buttons to move
  2. Collect data to earn points
  3. Collect all data to unlock the exit
  4. Reach the exit before time runs out

Game Features

  • There are three levels with increasing difficulty
  • There is a points system
  • Leaderboard to compare high scores
